# Bobo Giggle Grove — permanent story-generation and review rules

## Lessons from Story 1 (2026-10-17)
The first Puddle Surprise render missed the explicit jump, single tiny droplet, and droplet-on-nose ending. Its spoken "Big splash!" began too early. A successful 11-second file and consistent Bobo appearance are NOT proof that the story is correct. Reusing a scene reference preserves visual identity but does not guarantee requested action.

## Required for every upcoming video
1. Create a detailed, shot-by-shot prompt for all 11 seconds BEFORE spending free GPU quota. Define visual setup, action, cause-and-effect, comedic payoff, timing, camera framing, prop movement, and exact Bobo reaction. Avoid vague phrases like "funny reaction" in place of explicit observable actions.
2. Include positive action requirements AND negative exclusions (no wrong splash, no scene changes, no extra characters, no unrelated props, no duplicated motion). Specify consistent 3D Bobo, golden-brown fur, large eyes and ears, red shirt, smooth motion, and relevant story costume.
3. Ensure each required beat is physically visible in frames, not merely described in prompt, subtitles or metadata. For story 1: a real jump, ONE rising droplet, visible contact with tip of nose, then cross-eyed comic reaction.
4. Place speech at the actual intended action or punchline; generate synchronized subtitle cues from measured audio duration. Verify mouth/action alignment, intelligibility, SFX and music levels.
5. Review actual video frames and audio, not just metadata, duration or successful workflow status. If beats are missing, report the exact defect and regenerate/repair; do not claim a pass.
6. Verify Facebook and Instagram separately; do not advance story queue until required publication IDs/links are confirmed. Do not duplicate publication.
7. Free-quota-only generation. Do not use paid services.
